# Review environments now need the VPN, and the group platform is ready for its first company

*Infrastructure news for November 2026 (2026-11-01 to 2026-11-27).*

**In 30 seconds**
- **Act:** use the VPN for the admin pages of review environments, and check your Ingress rules before the
  Traefik switch.
- **Coming:** review environments move to a new cluster, and GitLab is being switched off. Still using GitLab?
  Tell the infra team.
- **Win:** shorter CI queues, and Teams alerts that come with logs, a graph and a silence button.

*Review environments* are the test copy of the Sdui app that is built for each ticket, on `sdux.de`.

## What you need to do

**Use the VPN for review-environment admin pages**
- *Who:* anyone who opens the RabbitMQ admin page or the ODS API of a review environment.
- *What:* connect to the company VPN first. Since 2026-11-19 these pages no longer answer from the open internet
  ([#49](https://github.com/7education/infra-ci-central/pull/49)).
- *Help:* no VPN access yet? Open a ticket in the Jira project INFRA.

**Check your Ingress rules before the Traefik switch**
- *Who:* teams that write their own Kubernetes Ingress rules (the rules that route web traffic to a service) for
  review environments.
- *What:* review environments are moving to a new traffic router, Traefik. Two kinds of rule will stop working:
  passing requests to PHP the FastCGI way, and regex paths without the setting `pathType: ImplementationSpecific`.
  To check, search your chart for `fastcgi` and for regex paths
  ([#47](https://github.com/7education/infra-ci-central/pull/47),
  [#48](https://github.com/7education/infra-ci-central/pull/48)).
- *Help:* a ticket in Jira INFRA.

Nothing else needs action from you.

## Where this is going

**Progress:** 8 companies have their own space on the group platform, and 1 (Fox) has its production database
ready there. Old servers switched off this month: the old Countly cluster and 8 more.

**One platform for the whole group.** The 7education group (Sdui, Additio, Educamos, Fox and others) is moving
onto one shared platform at OVH, a European cloud provider. It runs apps on Kubernetes and databases (PostgreSQL
and MariaDB) on shared servers. Companies with a space there today: 7os, Additio, Digionline, DS42, Educamos,
Fox, the group's internal IT, and Sdui. Fox's new database replaces 10 database servers at another cloud
provider. If your company still runs its own database servers, the infra team can take over patching, backups
and restore tests. Ask in Jira INFRA ([#629](https://github.com/7education/services-ovh-infra/pull/629)).

**Backups proven by restores (Dev now, production next).** A database on the group platform goes live only after
its backup was restored successfully once. After that, one backup is restored every week. The infra team runs
this for you; ask them to include it in your go-live date
([#670](https://github.com/7education/services-ovh-infra/pull/670),
[#1172](https://github.com/7education/services-ovh-infra/pull/1172)).

**Fewer old servers.** The infra team switched off the old cluster of Countly (the app usage analytics tool) and
8 old Sdui servers: phpMyAdmin, the timetable web server, two Redis workers, a preprod jump host, and the s3sync,
imaginary and broadcast services. That is less to pay for and less to patch
([#71](https://github.com/7education/Infrastructure/pull/71),
[#75](https://github.com/7education/Infrastructure/pull/75),
[#110](https://github.com/7education/Infrastructure/pull/110)).

## Good to know

- **Shorter CI queues.** Repos that build on the group platform's Dev runners can run 100 jobs at once instead of
  58, so jobs wait less ([#1048](https://github.com/7education/services-ovh-infra/pull/1048)).
- **Alerts you can act on.** Alerts in Teams now include logs, pod status, a graph, and buttons to silence or
  acknowledge them ([#1080](https://github.com/7education/services-ovh-infra/pull/1080)). Want your service's
  alerts in your team's channel? Open a PR in `Infrastructure`.
